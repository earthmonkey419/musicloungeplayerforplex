Help

A quick guide to everything MusicLounge Player can do.

ON THIS PAGE

SEARCH

Type into the search bar on the Player home page to search your whole library as you type. Results load a page at a time as you scroll, so searching a large library stays fast.

Below the search bar, the genre pills (Funk, Soul, Jazz, and so on) work as one-tap mood browsing — no typing needed. If your library is connected to MusicMind, you'll also see an "Instrumental only" toggle for filtering out vocal tracks within a mood.

On any result, track, or playlist row: ▶ plays it now, ⏬ adds it to your queue without interrupting what's currently playing, and + adds it to a playlist.

BROWSE

The Browse tab is for exploring your library by structure rather than searching for something specific. Toggle between Artists and Albums, and sort either one (name, recently added, and for albums, also by artist or year).

Tap an artist to see their albums, newest first. Tap an album — from either the artist page or directly from the Albums view — to see its full tracklist with the same play/queue/playlist actions as search results, plus a Play Album button to queue up the whole thing in track order.

PLAYLISTS

Playlists come in two kinds:

Native playlists live entirely in MusicLounge Player. Build them by adding tracks from search or Browse, reorder them by dragging, and remove tracks any time. When you're happy with one, Export to Plex creates a matching playlist in your actual Plex library (named with a MusicLounge- prefix, so it's always obvious which playlists came from here).

Plex-synced playlists are existing playlists already in your Plex library, imported for convenient access here. These stay read-only and always refresh to match Plex when you view them — edit the original in Plex if you want to change what's in it.

QUEUE & NOW PLAYING

Your queue persists — it survives page reloads and navigating around the app, so you never lose your place. Tap the ☰ button in the player bar to see the full queue: tap any track to jump to it, drag to reorder, or remove tracks you don't want anymore.

Tap the album art or track title/artist in the player bar (not the buttons) to open the expanded Now Playing view — large art, a real progress bar you can drag to seek to any point in the track, and full transport controls.

Playback itself doesn't stop when you navigate between Player, Browse, and Playlists — it's one continuous listening session across all three.

ROOM MODE

Room Mode turns MusicLounge Player into a shared jukebox. Start a room from the Player home page, and you'll get a join code and QR code to share — anyone who joins adds to one shared queue, and everyone sees the same now-playing track, no account needed on their end.

Audio plays through your device (whoever started the room) — guests are choosing what plays, not streaming it themselves. This makes it well suited for an actual shared space: a party, an office, a car full of people passing a phone around.

SHARE MODE

Share Mode creates a private, time-limited link to a track, album, artist, or playlist — for sending to one specific person rather than hosting a live session. Pick how long the link should stay valid, then copy it or (if SMTP is configured) email it directly.

Whoever opens the link gets their own simple player for that content — it plays on their device, and doesn't touch your own queue or listening history.

LAST.FM SCROBBLING

If your instance has Last.fm configured, connect your account once from Settings. After that, tracks you listen to in Player, Playlists, or Browse show up as "now playing" and get scrobbled automatically once you've genuinely listened to enough of them — Last.fm's own rule, not something skipped tracks trigger.

Room Mode and Share Mode plays are never scrobbled, even when connected — those aren't necessarily your own personal listening.

INSTALL AS AN APP

MusicLounge Player can be added to your phone's home screen for a real app-like experience — its own icon, and no browser address bar when you open it.

iOS (Safari): tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen."

Android (Chrome): tap the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app."
